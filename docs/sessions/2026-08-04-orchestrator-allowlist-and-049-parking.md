## Why a second recap — and the structural cause

This is the second separate-docs-PR recap in one day, a pattern
`docs/AGENT_PLAYBOOK.md` retired. The first was a sequencing error, recorded
as deviation 2 in the earlier recap. This one is not: the earlier recap had
already merged before this work existed, so there was no branch left to ride.

The common cause is that recap transport assumes **one task per session**. A
recap rides its task's PR. This session ran four units of work — the process
map, its recap, the allowlist fix, and the 049 brief — and no recap can ride
four PRs, least of all one that merged first.

By the escalation in `AGENT_PLAYBOOK.md` chapter 5, a second occurrence is
when a pattern becomes a rule. The candidate rule is not "stop running
multi-unit sessions" — the owner drove all four deliberately — but a recap
clause covering the case: either a session recap that ships last on its own
branch by design, or an explicit one-unit-per-session constraint with the
Orchestrator stopping when a second unit opens.
